## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **Dr. G. Venkataswamy (Dr. V)**, b. 1918, Tamil Nadu; MD Stanley Medical
  College 1944.
- **Rheumatoid arthritis** from ~1948; bedridden over a year, fingers too
  crippled to hold a pen; relearned to hold a scalpel and became a cataract
  surgeon.
- Founded **Aravind Eye Hospital in 1976 at age 58** (GOVEL Trust) with an
  **11-bed clinic**, no money or business plan. Mission: end needless blindness.
- **McDonald's model**: high-volume, high-quality, low-cost, standardized
  cataract surgery; **100+ surgeries a day**, **100,000+** in his lifetime.
- **Cross-subsidy**: ~**60% of services free or steeply subsidised**, yet
  financially self-reliant with a surplus; no one turned away.
- Grew into the **world's largest eye-care network**; sight restored to millions.
Sources: Aravind Eye Care System, Wikipedia, CRSToday, PMC/NCBI.
