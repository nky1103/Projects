## Why overlays instead of letting Gemini render the text
Veo/Gemini mangles exact text, numbers and logos. Generate only the **studio
ambiance + voiceover** with Gemini; keep the brand furniture, the ₹107 Cr and the
@statupsutra handle as these overlays so every fact and mark is correct and
on-brand.
