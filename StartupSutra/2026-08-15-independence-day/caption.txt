Freedom to build. 🇮🇳

In 1947, India won the freedom to choose its own path. Today, a new generation is using that freedom to build one.

Every founder who takes on a hard problem instead of an easy one. Every team that chooses to make it in India instead of importing it. Every idea that refuses to wait for permission. That is independence, in action.

To everyone building a more self-reliant India, one idea at a time: keep going. We're rooting for you.
