# 🔴 STARTUP FAILURE #003 — Koo — Mon 2026-08-03

Brief **Startup Failure #003** (Monday). Editorial post-mortem (red `#ef4444`).
**7 slides + 1 reel + Stories.** Editorial thesis: **a moment is not a moat,
growth you don't control, with no path to revenue, is rented, not owned.**

**Subject:** Koo, India's made-in-India, multilingual Twitter rival that hit
60M+ downloads and a ~$275M valuation, then shut down in July 2024, because it
was built on the 2021 Twitter-India moment, never cracked monetization, and had
no business underneath the buzz.
**Framework:** Build the business, not just the buzz.

## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- Founded **2019** by **Aprameya Radhakrishna** & **Mayank Bidawatka**;
  multilingual, made-in-India microblogging.
- Shot up in **2021** after the India government vs Twitter standoff; ministers
  and celebrities joined; ~**60M+ downloads**.
- Peak valuation **~$275M** (Nov 2022); raised **~$50.4M** total (Tiger Global,
  Accel, Blume, 3one4).
- Never cracked monetization; social media's server + moderation costs are
  heavy. Funding winter → merger/buyout talks collapsed (buyers didn't want the
  cost/liability of moderating UGC) → salaries unpaid from April 2024 → **shut
  down July 3, 2024**.
Sources: TechCrunch, Forbes, Wikipedia, The Secretariat.
