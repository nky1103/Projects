The Rise

In 2021, India clashed with Twitter. Koo exploded overnight.

Ministers, celebrities and lakhs of users jumped to the "made in India" app in a wave of patriotic momentum. Downloads went vertical. It looked unstoppable.

#003 · 06/07
The End

Funding froze. Buyers walked away.

No one wanted the cost and liability of moderating user content. Merger talks collapsed. Salaries went unpaid from April 2024. Koo shut down in July 2024.
The buzz was real. The business underneath never was.

The Lesson

A moment is not a moat.

Growth you don't control, with no path to revenue, is rented, not owned. Build the business, not just the buzz. 💬 Did you ever actually use Koo? YES or NO.
