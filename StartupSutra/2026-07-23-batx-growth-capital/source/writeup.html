BatX Energies raised

Series · Growth Capital (Thu) Date · 23 Jul 2026 Subject · BatX Energies, Gurugram Framework · Fund the shovel-maker

01 Carousel caption (posts 08:00 IST)

They didn't back another EV startup. They backed the one mining dead batteries. BatX Energies (Gurugram) just raised ₹105 Cr in a Series A led by IvyCap Ventures. India's EV and energy-storage boom runs on lithium, cobalt and nickel. And India imports almost all of it, mostly from China. BatX recovers those exact minerals from dead batteries and factory scrap. Its battery recycling turns waste into a domestic supply of critical minerals. It's already commercial, and it's moving up the chain into refining and cathode materials. That's the signal investors bought. Not "recycling is green," but "we can make the scarce input everyone will need." The lesson isn't about batteries: In a gold rush, capital funds the shovel-maker. Don't crowd the booming market.
