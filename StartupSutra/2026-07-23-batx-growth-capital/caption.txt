They didn't back another EV startup. They backed the one mining dead batteries.

BatX Energies (Gurugram) just raised ₹105 Cr in a Series A led by IvyCap Ventures.

India's EV and energy-storage boom runs on lithium, cobalt and nickel — and India imports almost all of it, mostly from China.

BatX recovers those exact minerals from dead batteries and factory scrap, turning waste into a domestic supply of strategic inputs. It's already commercial, and it's moving up the chain into refining and cathode materials.

That's the signal investors bought: not "recycling is green," but "we can make the scarce input everyone will need."

The lesson isn't about batteries:

In a gold rush, capital funds the shovel-maker. Don't crowd the booming market — own the scarce input that feeds it.
