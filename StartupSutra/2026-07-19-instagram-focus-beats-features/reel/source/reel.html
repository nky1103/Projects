Imagine deleting 80% of your startup…

…and becoming a billion-dollar company.

Before Instagram…

Kevin Systrom built an app called Burbn.

Burbn had:

📍Check-ins
🎮Gaming
👥Social networking
📸Photo sharing

The problem?

Nobody used most of it.

But one feature exploded…

📸Photo sharing

So instead of adding more…

They deleted almost everything.

Two months later…

0

users. 🚀

Sometimes the fastest way to grow…

is to remove more.
