Imagine deleting eighty percent of your startup… and becoming a billion-dollar company.

Before Instagram, Kevin Systrom built an app called Burbn.

It had check-ins, gaming, social networking, and photo sharing.

The problem? Nobody used most of it.

But one feature exploded: photo sharing.

So instead of adding more… they deleted almost everything.

Two months later, Instagram had one million users.

Sometimes the fastest way to grow… is to remove more.

Focus beats features.
