Instagram wasn't Kevin Systrom's first idea.

Before Instagram, he built an app called Burbn.

It had check-ins, gaming, social networking, and photo sharing.

Sound familiar?

Like many first-time founders, he tried to solve too many problems at once.

But when users started using only one feature—photo sharing—he made a bold decision.

He deleted almost everything else.

That single decision became Instagram.

Lesson:
The best products aren't always the ones with the most features.
They're the ones that solve one problem exceptionally well.

💬 Which startup do you think became successful by doing less?
