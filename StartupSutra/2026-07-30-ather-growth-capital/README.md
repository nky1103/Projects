## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **₹1,300 Cr QIP** (~21 Jul 2026): 1.08 Cr equity shares at **₹1,202** each.
  Buyers >5% each: **HDFC MF, Aditya Birla SL MF, Axis MF, Tata MF, Motilal
  Oswal MF, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority.**
- Part of a **₹2,500 Cr** board-approved plan (12 Jun 2026: ₹1,500 Cr QIP +
  ₹1,000 Cr equity-linked). First major raise since the **April 2025 IPO**
  (₹2,981 Cr, ₹321/share).
- **Q2 FY26**: revenue ₹898.9 Cr, **up 54% YoY**; net loss **₹154.1 Cr**,
  narrowed from ₹197.2 Cr.
- EV two-wheeler **market share 17.4%**, up from 12.1% in Q2 FY25.
- Still unprofitable; competes with Ola, TVS, Bajaj on price.
Sources: StartupTalky, Business Standard, Tracxn, TechStory, Multibagg.
