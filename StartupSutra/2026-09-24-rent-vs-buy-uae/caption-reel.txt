In New York, a home costs about 22x its annual rent. In London, about 27x. In Dubai, about 15x.
A lower price-to-rent ratio means rent pays the property off faster.
Dubai's ratio sits among the lowest of any major global city, which is why owning here starts to beat renting sooner than it does almost anywhere else.
