The Promise

Only a few Indians have a credit card. ZestMoney gave everyone credit.

Buy now, pay in instalments, no card required. It democratised credit for millions the banks ignored. At its peak: a $450M valuation and Goldman Sachs money.

#004 · 05/07
The Trigger

In 2022, one RBI notification gutted the model.

The regulator barred non-banks from loading credit lines onto prepaid instruments. Overnight, the core of the BNPL business was cut off. ZestMoney had no say, and no plan B.

#004 · 06/07
The End

A rescue buyer walked. Then it was over.

PhonePe was set to buy it for $200 to 300 million, then pulled out after due diligence. The founders stepped down. In 2023 to 2024, ZestMoney shut down, from a $450M peak to zero.
The users were real. The foundation was borrowed.
