## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- BNPL fintech, Goldman Sachs-backed; founded **2015** by **Lizzie Chapman,
  Priya Sharma, Ashish Anantharaman.** Democratised credit without a card.
- Peak: **$450M valuation (2021)**, 500+ employees.
- **RBI (June 2022)**: barred non-bank prepaid issuers from loading credit
  lines, gutting the BNPL model.
- High defaults / weak collections; unit economics broke.
- **PhonePe** planned to acquire (~$200-300M), **called it off** after due
  diligence (early 2023); founders stepped down (May 2023); **shutdown announced
  Dec 2023**, wound down 2024 (assets/brand to DMI Finance).
Sources: Inc42, Entrepreneur India, Business Standard, Forbes India, Dazeinfo.
