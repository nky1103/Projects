They didn't invest in plastic. They invested in replacing it.

BZERO (Valencia) just closed an oversubscribed €1.5M seed round for a home-compostable film that's 7 microns thin — and performs like the low-density plastic it replaces, at the same cost.

That last part is the whole story.

The breakthrough wasn't sustainability. Plenty of "eco" materials exist. They're just more expensive, so companies don't switch.

BZERO matched the cost and the strength — and made switching a no-brainer.

That's what investors actually buy: not a better product, but a business model people can adopt without a reason to say no.

Investors rarely fund "better." They fund inevitable.
