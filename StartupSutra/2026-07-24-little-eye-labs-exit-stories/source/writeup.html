Exit Stories #001

Little Eye Labs. Facebook's first India deal. The full write-up.

Series · Exit Stories (Fri) Date · 24 Jul 2026 Subject · Little Eye Labs, Bengaluru Framework · Own the capability

01 Carousel caption (posts 08:00 IST)

Facebook's first-ever acquisition in India wasn't a big consumer app. It was a 13-person team in Bengaluru. In January 2014, Facebook bought Little Eye Labs for a reported $10 to $15 million. Little Eye solved a boring, painful problem in mobile app performance. In 2013, an Android app was a black box. You couldn't see why one drained the battery, ate data, or lagged. Plug any Android phone into a laptop and Little Eye showed CPU, memory, battery and data in real time, tied to what the app was doing. Zero code. Here's why Facebook cared. That year, 874 million of its 1.19 billion users were on mobile. Android ran 80% of the world's phones. Facebook needed lighter, faster apps for the next billion people. Little Eye wasn't 13 people. It was a capability Facebook couldn't build fast enough. That's the startup exit lesson. Great acquisitions aren't about your size. They're about the problem you own. Don't build to get bought.
