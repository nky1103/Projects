# 🌹 EXIT STORIES #001 — Little Eye Labs — Fri 2026-07-24

**New series debut** (rose `#f43f5e`, Fridays): *what an exit teaches*. Built per
the Editorial Bible v1.0 + Creative Brief #002.

**Subject:** Little Eye Labs (Bengaluru) — Facebook's first-ever India
acquisition, January 2014, reported $10–15M. A 13-person team (5 founders, GSF
Accelerator batch) whose Android performance tooling Facebook needed for its
mobile pivot.
**Framework:** *Acquirers buy the capability you own, not the size of your team.*

## Story spine (Company → Principle → Framework)
- **Company:** Little Eye made a desktop tool that plugged into any Android phone
  over USB and showed CPU, memory, battery and data live, correlated to app
  activity. Zero code.
- **Why Facebook:** In 2013, 874M of its 1.19B users were on mobile; Android ran
  ~80% of the world's phones. It needed lighter, faster apps for the next
  billion. Little Eye *was* that capability (FB's Subbu Subramanian said so).
- **Lesson:** Great acquisitions are driven by strategic capability, not size.
  Build to own a problem someone bigger will one day need solved.
