# StartupSutra — Weekly Stories Playbook

**Why this exists:** zero Stories in 30 days was the single biggest unforced
error. Stories are the cheapest way to (a) warm the first-hour audience and
(b) generate the interaction data the algorithm learns from. Rule: **one teaser
1–2h before the post, one yes/no or poll follow-up right after.** Every day.

Post the teaser as a Story with a **poll or "This or That" sticker** where noted,
and the follow-up as an **emoji-slider / yes-no poll** so it's one tap to answer.
Keep the brand look: navy background, series-colour accent, owl.

---

## Sunday — Case Study 🟧 (Attero)
- ⏰ **Teaser (1–2h before):** "Tomorrow: the case study of a company everyone got wrong 👀"
- 💬 **Follow-up (right after):** "Did you know Attero isn't really a recycler? — Yes / No"
- ➕ Optional: quote sticker of the reversal — "It's actually a technology company."

## Monday — Startup Failure 🔴 (Quibi)
- ⏰ **Teaser:** "Tomorrow: how the most-funded startup of 2020 died in 6 months 👀"
- 💬 **Follow-up:** "Did you know COVID wasn't what killed Quibi? — Yes / No"
- ➕ Optional poll: "Would you have invested in Quibi? — Yes / No"

## Tuesday — Marketing Breakdown 🔵 (`[BRAND]` — Duolingo-style "isn't doing X, it's doing Y")
- ⏰ **Teaser (poll, guess-the-brand):** "Which brand stopped making ads and started making a *show*? — 🦉 / 🍔 / 👟"
- 💬 **Follow-up:** "Would you have guessed `[BRAND]`? — Yes / No"
- ➕ Note (from the plan): run the guess-the-brand poll **before** the reel drops.

## Wednesday — Seed Signal 🟣 (BZERO — rebuilt as a reel this week)
- ⏰ **Teaser:** "Tomorrow: the one signal that made investors say yes 👀"
- 💬 **Follow-up:** "Could you spot this signal before the raise? — Yes / No"
- ➕ **Behind-the-scenes Story (same day):** "How I research a funding round" — 2–3 frames of your process. Builds authority, low effort.

## Thursday — Growth Capital 🟩 (funding — lead with the number)
- ⏰ **Teaser (number):** "Tomorrow: someone just raised ₹`[__]` Cr. Guess what for 👀"
- 💬 **Follow-up:** "Would you have backed this round? — Yes / No"
- ➕ Optional "This or That": "Bigger opportunity: `[A]` or `[B]`?"

## Friday — Exit Stories 🌹 (your top-performing pillar — give it the most polish)
- ⏰ **Teaser (comment-bait, number):** "Tomorrow: a startup nobody's heard of got acquired for $`[__]` 👀 Guess the deal size."
- 💬 **Follow-up (slider):** "Was the deal size higher or lower than you guessed? — Higher / Lower"
- ➕ CTA to carry into the reel/caption: "Guess the deal size before I reveal it."

## Saturday — Founder Framework 🟢 (Kalpana — retention-first, outcome-led hook)
- ⏰ **Teaser:** "Tomorrow: she saved a company everyone had given up on. Guess how 👀"
- 💬 **Follow-up:** "Could you have done what she did? — Yes / No"
- ➕ Note: lead the reel/hook with the **outcome** ("She saved a company everyone gave up on"), not the backstory.

---

## Daily, regardless of pillar
- Post **at least one Story** (teaser, poll, or behind-the-scenes).
- Keep a **bio CTA/link** live so profile visits can convert.
- Track **reach + interactions** in Insights daily. Change **one variable at a
  time** (consolidation / reversal hook / Story warm-up) so you can attribute
  what actually moved the number by day 7.

## Reusable teaser formulas (swap the subject)
- "Tomorrow: the `[company]` everyone got wrong 👀"
- "Tomorrow: how `[company]` `[surprising outcome]` 👀"
- "Guess the `[brand / deal size / signal]` before I reveal it."
## Reusable follow-up formulas (one-tap)
- "Did you know this before today? — Yes / No"
- "Would you have `[invested / backed / guessed]`? — Yes / No"
- "Higher or lower than you guessed? — Higher / Lower"
