## 2. Background music

**Reel (priority for reach):** use a **trending audio** from Instagram's Reels
library — trending tracks get an algorithmic lift, and the reel is silent so the
music can sit at ~70–80% volume. In the audio picker, sort by the ↑ trending
arrow and choose a clean, modern, upbeat instrumental (no distracting lyrics).
Vibe to match the brand: "aesthetic / clean corporate / confident-energetic."
Avoid heavy pop vocals that fight the on-screen text.
- Fallback (brand-consistent, less reach): a minimal cinematic / soft lo-fi bed.

**Carousel:** optional. Instagram lets you add music to photo posts — a low-key
lo-fi or "aesthetic" instrumental at low volume works. Many carousels are read
in silence, so this is nice-to-have, not required.

> Rule of thumb: on Reels, a *current* trending sound beats a "perfect" one for
> reach. Refresh the pick each week — trending audio turns over fast.

The post is **complimentary**, and Duolingo's social team is famous for engaging
with mentions. Upside (a like/reshare to their millions) is huge; downside is
basically nil.

How to do it tastefully:
- **Tag @duolingo in the first comment** (keeps the caption clean), or work it
  into the caption naturally.

  tag @duolingo there — brand accounts reshare positive Story mentions far more
