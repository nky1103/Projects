Marketing Breakdown #002 · Tuesday

Zomato. A food app that became a media brand.

Series · Marketing Breakdown (Tue)Date · 28 Jul 2026Subject · ZomatoFramework · Your product is a media channel

01 Carousel caption (posts 08:00 IST)

Zomato isn't selling you food. It's selling a personality you follow. You've screenshotted a food-app notification. You laughed at it. You sent it to a friend. That's not luck. That's the whole strategy. Here's the move most brands miss. Zomato took its most boring surfaces and turned them into content. The push notification stopped being "your order is on the way" and became a joke you screenshot. The app copy got a voice. The social replies became memes remixed with a food twist. Why it works: a notification is a channel you already own. It's free, daily, and opt-in. Most brands waste it. Zomato made it a punchline you share. And it sells. During peak hours, those witty notifications drove roughly 10% more orders on their own. The joke isn't a distraction from the business. It is the business. The principle: the best marketing isn't a campaign. It's making every touchpoint worth sharing. The framework: your product is your biggest media channel.
