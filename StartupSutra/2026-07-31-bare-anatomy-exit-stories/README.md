## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **L'Oréal** signed (18 Jun 2026) to acquire a **majority stake** in
  **Innovist**; reported value **$350–450M** (₹3,240–4,170 Cr), officially
  undisclosed. Brands join L'Oréal's Consumer Products Division.
- **Innovist** founded **2019** by **Rohit Chawla, Sifat Khurana, Vimal Bhola**;
  science-led, digital-first house of brands: **Bare Anatomy, Chemist at Play,
  Sunscoop.** In-house R&D + manufacturing.
- Founding team **stays on as minority shareholders**; L'Oréal has an option to
  fully buy out the minority later.
- Funding: pre-Series A $3.5M (Accel, 72 Ventures); Series A Nov 2023 ₹58 Cr
  (Amazon Smbhav, 72 Ventures = Falguni Nayar / Sanjay Nayar family office,
  Accel, Sauce VC); Series B ₹136 Cr led by **ICICI Venture**.
Sources: L'Oréal Finance press release, Entrackr, StartupTalky, Inc42,
Cosmetics Business.
