2. Snapshot: Innovist, founded 2019 in India by Rohit Chawla, Sifat Khurana and Vimal Bhola, owner of Bare Anatomy, Chemist at Play and Sunscoop, backed by ICICI Venture, Accel, Amazon and Falguni Nayar's family office.
3. The setup: India's beauty buyer changed. She buys online, reads ingredient labels and trusts native brands over legacy ones, and the giants could not keep up.
4. What they built: not one brand but a house of brands, Bare Anatomy, Chemist at Play and Sunscoop, with clean formulations, in-house R&D and manufacturing, sold direct to a young online audience.
5. Why L'Oréal bought in: seven years from launch to a $350 million-plus valuation, three fast-growing digital-first brands, in-house R&D, and an instant foothold with India's young online shopper while the founders stay on.
6. Founder lesson: L'Oréal did not buy shampoo, it bought a digital-native brand and audience it could not build fast. Incumbents buy trust with a new generation.
