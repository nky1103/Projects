India Inc used to be the one that got bought. Tata just wrote a 3.8 billion euro cheque to buy an 80-year-old European truckmaker.

In July 2025, Tata Motors agreed to acquire Italy's Iveco for roughly 3.8 billion euros, all cash. For decades, a global deal meant a foreign giant buying an Indian company. This one runs the other way.

The two barely overlap, so together they build around 540,000 trucks a year. Iveco's owners get a clean exit. Tata gets 80 years of European brand, dealers and factories it could not build from scratch.

Real independence is when you stop getting bought and start buying.
