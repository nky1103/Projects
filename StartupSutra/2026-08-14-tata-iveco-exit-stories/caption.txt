India Inc used to be the one that got bought. Tata just wrote a 3.8 billion euro cheque to buy an 80-year-old European truckmaker.

In July 2025, Tata Motors agreed to acquire Italy's Iveco Group for roughly 3.8 billion euros, all cash. The deal is expected to close in 2026.

For decades, a "global deal" meant a foreign giant buying an Indian company. This one runs the other way.

Why it fits: the two barely overlap. Tata is strong in India and the Americas, Iveco in Europe. Together, around 540,000 trucks a year. Fewer plants to shut, more markets to add.

Iveco's owners get a clean cash exit. Tata gets 80 years of European brand, dealers and factories it could not build from scratch in a decade.

The lesson: the exit game has two seats. India Inc is learning to sit on the buyer's side. That is what independence looks like.
