## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **Tata Motors → Iveco Group**, announced **30 Jul 2025.** ~**€3.8 billion**, all
  cash, at **€14.1 per share** (a ~22 to 25% premium). Financing via a Morgan
  Stanley + MUFG consortium.
- Expected to **close in 2026** (by 31 Mar 2026 / H1 2026).
- **Iveco's defence business** (IDV, ASTRA) is carved out and sold separately to
  **Leonardo** (~€1.7B EV); Tata buys the commercial-vehicle business.
- Combined: ~**€22B** revenue, ~**540k** units a year, split across Europe (~50%),
  India (~35%) and the Americas (~15%), with little geographic overlap.
Sources: Tata Motors / Iveco Group press releases, Reuters, autoevolution.
