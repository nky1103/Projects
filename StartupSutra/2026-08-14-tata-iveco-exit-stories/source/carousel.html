The Exit Logic

Both sides get the half they were missing.

Iveco's owners get a clean, full-price cash exit and a new home for the business. Tata gets instant global scale it could not buy off a spec sheet. Buying reach beats spending ten years earning it.
The strongest exit is the one that makes the buyer bigger than before.
