boAt doesn't really sell earphones. It sells an identity. And along the way, it freed India from overpriced foreign audio.

In 2016, Bose, JBL and Sony set the price and the vibe. Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta started boAt with just ₹30 lakh and a different idea: don't sell specs, sell a cool, sporty, made-for-India lifestyle. Buyers weren't customers, they were "boAtheads".

Instead of pouring crores into TV ads, boAt built a culture machine: 500+ influencers, cricketers, musicians, and a founder who became a Shark Tank star. Design in India, stay asset-light, and let the community do the marketing.

The result: from ₹31 crore in year one to over ₹4,000 crore in FY25, the world's 5th-largest wearable brand, and number one in audio in India.

The framework: don't sell the product, sell the identity people want to wear. Then price it for your market, and let the community do the ads.
