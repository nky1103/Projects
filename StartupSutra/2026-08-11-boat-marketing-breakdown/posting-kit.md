Cover (keyword-loaded, for Instagram Edit → alt text):
StartupSutra Marketing Breakdown on boAt, the made-in-India audio and wearables brand co-founded by Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta that sells an identity, not earphones. A brand-strategy case study of how boAt freed Indian buyers from overpriced foreign brands with made-for-India pricing, a lifestyle "boAtheads" community, 500-plus influencers and cricket and music tie-ins instead of TV ads, growing from ₹31 crore to over ₹4,000 crore in FY25 to become the world's fifth-largest wearable brand. A marketing and Made-in-India lesson for founders.

1. Marketing Breakdown, boAt. It doesn't really sell earphones, it sells an identity.
2. The setup: in 2016 foreign brands like Bose, JBL and Sony owned audio at foreign prices; Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta started boAt with just ₹30 lakh and a different idea.
3. The move: don't sell specs, sell a cool, sporty, made-for-India lifestyle; buyers became "boAtheads" and audio became a fashion accessory, not a spec sheet.
4. The engine: skip the TV crores and build a culture machine, 500-plus influencers, cricketers, musicians and a founder who became a Shark Tank star, designing in India and staying asset-light.
5. The payoff: from ₹31 crore in year one to over ₹4,000 crore in FY25, the world's 5th-largest wearable brand, 10 million-plus products made in India, and number one in audio in India.
6. The principle: it freed India from overpriced foreign audio with the same aspiration at an Indian price and vibe, beating global giants by being made for you.
7. The framework: don't sell the product, sell the identity people want to wear, then price it for your market and let the community do the ads. Follow @statupsutra, Marketing Breakdown on Tuesdays.
