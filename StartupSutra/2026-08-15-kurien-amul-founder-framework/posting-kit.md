## Reel caption (reversal hook)
Everyone hands charity to the poor. This man handed farmers ownership instead, and it freed millions.

Verghese Kurien reached the village of Anand in 1949 as an engineer who never wanted the job. Farmers did the work, middlemen kept the money. So he built a business the farmers themselves owned: Amul, a co-operative where every producer is a shareholder, owning the milk, the dairy and the brand. Scaled through Operation Flood, it made India the world's largest milk producer, with about 36 lakh farmer-owners today.

1. Founder Framework, Verghese Kurien and Amul. He was sent to a dusty village to repay a loan and wanted to quit and leave; he stayed and freed millions of farmers.
2. Snapshot: Verghese Kurien, the Milkman of India, built Amul and Operation Flood, starting in Anand, Gujarat in 1949, on the idea of letting farmers own the business.
3. The struggle: farmers did the work while middlemen kept the money, as private traders set the price, and Kurien, a foreign-trained engineer who never wanted the job, chose to fight an entrenched monopoly with almost no money and no blueprint.
4. Framework one, give ownership to the people who do the work: don't fight the middleman, replace him with the farmer, so Kurien built a co-operative owned by the milk producers where every farmer was a shareholder and the profit came home to the village.
5. Framework two, own the whole chain not just the cow: instead of the farmer selling cheap while a trader keeps the margin, the Amul way has farmers own the dairy and the brand, collecting, processing and marketing milk so the value stays with the village.
6. The impact: about 36 lakh farmer-owners in the Amul family today, India became the world's largest milk producer, milk output rose from about 23 million to over 250 million tonnes a year since Operation Flood, across thousands of farmer-owned village co-ops.
7. The framework: real freedom is owning what you produce, because Kurien handed farmers ownership rather than charity and let them build their own way out. Follow @statupsutra, Founder Framework on Saturdays.
