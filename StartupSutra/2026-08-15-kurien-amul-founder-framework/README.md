## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **Verghese Kurien** (1921 to 2012), "Milkman of India," Father of the White
  Revolution. Reached **Anand, Gujarat in 1949** on a government bond; nearly
  left. Stayed at the request of co-op founder **Tribhuvandas Patel.**
- Built the farmer-owned **Amul** co-operative (GCMMF; the co-op body formed
  **1946**). Chairman of **NDDB** (1965 to 1998) and **GCMMF** (1973 to 2006).
- **Operation Flood** (launched **1970**) scaled the Anand model nationwide and
  made **India the world's largest milk producer**; output rose from ~**23M**
  tonnes/year (late 1960s) to over **250M** tonnes today.
- The Amul family holds roughly **36 lakh (3.6 million) farmer-owners** today,
  across thousands of village co-operatives. Co-founders include Tribhuvandas
  Patel and **H. M. Dalaya.** World Food Prize 1989; Padma Vibhushan.
Sources: NDDB, GCMMF (Amul), World Food Prize, Britannica.
