He was sent to a dusty village to repay a loan. He wanted to quit. He stayed, and freed millions of farmers.

Verghese Kurien, the Milkman of India, reached Anand in 1949 as an engineer who never wanted the job. Farmers did the work while middlemen kept the money.

His answer was not charity. It was ownership. He built Amul, a co-operative owned by the farmers themselves. Scaled through Operation Flood, it made India the world's largest milk producer, with about 36 lakh farmer-owners today.

Real freedom is owning what you produce.
