#004 · 03/07
The Struggle

Farmers did the work. Middlemen kept the money.

Private traders set the price, and paid farmers a pittance for their milk. Kurien, a foreign-trained engineer who never wanted this job, chose to fight an entrenched monopoly with almost no money and no blueprint.

#004 · 04/07
1Give ownership to the people who do the work

Don't fight the middleman. Replace him with the farmer.

Kurien built a co-operative owned by the milk producers themselves. Every farmer, however small, was a shareholder. The profit stopped going to a trader and started coming home to the village.
