1. Founder Framework, Kalpana Saroj. She was forced into marriage at 12 and later saved a company everyone had given up on. Chairperson of Kamani Tubes, Padma Shri.
2. The beginning: a Dalit girl in a village in Maharashtra, facing poverty, caste discrimination and child marriage at 12. Most assumed her story was already written.
3. Starting again: she reached Mumbai, did garment work for as little as 2 rupees a day, and in 1984 used a 50,000 rupee loan to start tailoring, then furniture. Rebuilding herself.
4. The biggest gamble: in 2001 Kamani Tubes, a 40-year-old metals maker, was bankrupt and in liquidation with 3,500 workers unpaid for nearly 3 years. The workers asked her to take it over.
5. The turnaround: she cleared the debt, settled workers' dues and restarted production. Today the KS Group spans six companies and thousands of crores in turnover.
6. The Founder Framework: Pain to Purpose to Persistence to Execution to Transformation.
