Kalpana Saroj. The turnaround was her own life.

Series · Founder Framework (Sat) Date · 25 Jul 2026 Subject · Kalpana Saroj, Kamani Tubes Framework · Pain → Transformation

01 Carousel caption (posts 08:00 IST)

Everyone celebrates billion-dollar founders. Very few talk about the woman who rebuilt a dying company after surviving child marriage, poverty and discrimination. Kalpana Saroj was married at 12, against her will. She left an abusive home, reached Mumbai, and worked in a garment factory for as little as ₹2 a day. In 1984, a ₹50,000 loan started a tailoring business, then furniture. She wasn't chasing success. She was rebuilding herself. Then came the gamble. In 2001, Kamani Tubes was bankrupt. A 40-year-old metals company in liquidation, with 3,500 workers unpaid for nearly three years. The workers asked her to take it over. She did. She cleared the debt, settled the dues, and restarted production. Today she chairs the KS Group, six companies turning over thousands of crores. In 2013, she received the Padma Shri. She didn't inherit an empire. She built one through resilience, courage and relentless execution. The Founder Framework: Pain → Purpose → Persistence → Execution → Transformation.
