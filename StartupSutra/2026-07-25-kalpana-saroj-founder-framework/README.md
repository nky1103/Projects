# 🟢 FOUNDER FRAMEWORK #001 — Kalpana Saroj — Sat 2026-07-25

**Brief SS-FF-001.** New series debut (green `#22c55e`): decode the transferable
framework behind a founder's journey. Format: documentary / emotional
storytelling. Positioned as a **business transformation against impossible
odds**, not "rags to riches" (per the editorial refinement in the brief).

**Subject:** Kalpana Saroj — Chairperson, Kamani Tubes / KS Group; Padma Shri
(2013). Married at 12; rebuilt from ₹2/day garment work in Mumbai; revived the
bankrupt Kamani Tubes in 2001 and saved 3,500 jobs.
**Framework:** Pain → Purpose → Persistence → Execution → Transformation.

## Emotion journey (per brief)
Curiosity → Empathy → Hope → Suspense → Triumph → Respect → Reflection, mapped
1:1 to slides 1–7 (chapter labels carry the documentary spine).

## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- b. 1961, Roperkheda village, Akola district, Maharashtra; Dalit family; father
  a police constable who kept her in school.
- Married at 12 against her will → Mumbai → abuse → returned; survived a suicide
  attempt as a teen.
- Mumbai garment work, ~₹2/day. 1984: ₹50,000 govt loan (backward-caste scheme)
  → tailoring, then furniture; repaid.
- 2001: **Kamani Tubes** (copper/brass maker, founded 1959) bankrupt, in
  liquidation, 3,500 workers unpaid ~3 years. She took over (~₹50 lakh), cleared
  debts, settled dues, restarted. Now Chairperson.
- **KS Group**: ~6 companies (Kamani Tubes, Kamani Steel, Saikrupa Sugar,
  Kalpana Builders & Developers, Kalpana Saroj & Associates, KS Film Production).
  Turnover cited in the thousands of crores; net worth cited ~₹900–1,000 cr.
- **Padma Shri, 2013** (Trade & Industry). BBC: "the original slumdog millionaire."
Sources: BBC, The Weekend Leader, Outlook Business, Wikipedia, india.com.
