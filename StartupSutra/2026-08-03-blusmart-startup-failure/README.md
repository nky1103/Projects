# 🔴 STARTUP FAILURE #003 — BluSmart — Mon 2026-08-03

Brief **Startup Failure #003** (Monday). Editorial post-mortem (red `#ef4444`).
**7 slides + 1 reel + Stories.** Editorial thesis: **a great product can't
survive bad governance, never let a related party control the one thing your
business depends on.**

**Subject:** BluSmart, India's all-electric ride-hailing service (an Uber rival)
that collapsed in April 2025, not because EVs don't work, but because it leased
its entire fleet from Gensol, a listed company run by the same co-founder, whose
EV loans SEBI alleged were diverted to promoter-linked entities.
**Framework:** Related-party dependency + weak governance = fatal, even for a
loved product.

## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- BluSmart suspended services **April 2025** across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru.
  All-electric, fixed fares, no surge, salaried drivers; ~**10,000 drivers**.
- BluSmart **leased its fleet from Gensol Engineering** (listed), run by
  co-founder **Anmol Jaggi** (also Gensol). Co-founders: Anmol Jaggi, Puneet
  Jaggi, Punit K Goyal.
- Gensol took **~₹978 Cr** loans (IREDA, PFC) to buy EVs. **SEBI alleged ~₹200
  Cr+ diverted** to promoter-linked entities (reportedly incl. a luxury flat at
  The Camellias). **SEBI barred the Jaggi brothers** from the securities market;
  forensic audit ongoing.
- Financing froze → BluSmart lost fleet access → couldn't operate → shut down.
Sources: SEBI orders, TechCrunch, The Tribune, Business Standard, Entrepreneur India.
