The Domino

Gensol froze. So BluSmart lost its cars.

Financing dried up, the fleet became inaccessible, and BluSmart simply couldn't operate. Services stopped. Around 10,000 drivers were stranded overnight.
The product was fine. The structure and governance killed it.

The Lesson

A great product can't survive bad governance.

Never let a related party control the one thing you depend on. Governance isn't paperwork, it's survival. 💬 Founder or structure, what killed BluSmart? One word.
