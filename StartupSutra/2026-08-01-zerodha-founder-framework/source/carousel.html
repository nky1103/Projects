2Make the product the marketing

Zero ad spend. Ever.

Instead of buying attention, Zerodha earned it. It taught India to invest with free education (Varsity) and answered every question in the open. Users became the marketing.
A product people can't stop recommending is cheaper than any ad, and it compounds.
