## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- Founded **2010**, Bengaluru, by **Nithin & Nikhil Kamath.** Name = "zero" +
  "rodha" (Sanskrit, barrier). **Never raised external capital.**
- **Flat ₹20 per order** discount broking; **free equity delivery.** Broke the
  percentage-fee model of legacy brokers.
- **Zero advertising spend**; grew via free education (Varsity), community and
  word of mouth.
- **FY24**: revenue **~₹9,993 Cr**, profit **~₹5,496 Cr** (~55% margin).
  1.6 Cr+ clients, ~15% of daily retail volumes.
- **Honest counterweight**: SEBI's F&O rule changes cooled trading; **Groww
  overtook Zerodha on active-client count**; **FY25 profit fell ~23%**
  (₹4,237 Cr). Framed in the deck, not hidden.
Sources: Business Standard, The Clueless Company, WorthScale, company filings.
