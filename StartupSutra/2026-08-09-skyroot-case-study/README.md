## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- **Vikram-1 reached orbit 18 Jul 2026** from Sriharikota, first private Indian
  rocket to orbit; India became the 3rd country (after US, China) with private
  orbital launch.
- Founders **Pawan Kumar Chandana** & **Naga Bharath Daka**, ex-ISRO, IIT alumni;
  founded Hyderabad **2018.**
- **May 2026: ~$60M** raised (Sherpalo Ventures + GIC), valuation **>$1.1B**,
  India's first space-tech unicorn. Ram Shriram on board.
- **>90% of Vikram-1 components made domestically**; vertically integrated (own
  engines, 3D-printed parts, avionics). 1,000+ employees; targeting ~1 launch/mo.
Sources: SpaceDaily, Wikipedia, SiliconIndia, Indian Defence News.
