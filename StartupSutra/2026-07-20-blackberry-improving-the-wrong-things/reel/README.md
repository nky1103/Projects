# Reel — 🔴 Startup Failure #001 (BlackBerry)

**File:** `reel-sf001-blackberry.mp4` — 1080×1920 (9:16), 25s, 30fps, SILENT (music added in-app) — first reel under the
locked Reel Philosophy (style guide §6).

## Concept
The reel is NOT the carousel. One idea only: *BlackBerry kept winning at the
wrong game.* No apps, no touchscreen, no security, no lesson — those live in
the carousel. The viewer leaves with one unanswered question ("What was the
mistake?") and one place to answer it (the carousel).

## Script
See `voiceover-script.txt` — the coffee-conversation voice ("You know what
fascinates me about BlackBerry?"). VO at natural pace; the pauses are the
delivery. Scene cuts land on breath boundaries via silence detection.

## Beat sheet (est., re-synced to VO)
- 0–3.5s  Cold open: "You know what fascinates me about BlackBerry?" (no branding; logo ~3s)
- 3.5–8s  "It didn't disappear because Apple made a better phone."
- 8–13.5s "It kept winning…" → "at the wrong game." (two beats)
- 13.5–16s "That's the scary part." (whisper slide, centered)
- 16–23s  "The thing you're best at… keeps you from seeing what's coming next."
- 23–26s  "BlackBerry learned that the hard way."
- 26–30s  End card: 🔴 pill + "The full story is in today's carousel." + follow
