# Reel — 🔴 Startup Failure #001 (BlackBerry)

**File:** `reel-sf001-blackberry.mp4` — 1080×1920 (9:16), 25s, 30fps, SILENT (music added in-app) — first reel under the
locked Reel Philosophy (style guide §6).

## Concept
The reel is NOT the carousel. One idea only: *BlackBerry kept winning at the
wrong game.* No apps, no touchscreen, no security, no lesson — those live in
the carousel. The viewer leaves with one unanswered question ("What was the
mistake?") and one place to answer it (the carousel).

## Script (silent cut — on-screen text)
- 0–2s    "Everyone says Apple killed BlackBerry." (instant, no branding; logo ~3.5s)
- 2–4s    "I don't think that's true."
- 4–10s   "BlackBerry kept building…" → "…a better keyboard."
- 10–16s  "Apple changed something bigger." → "What people expected from a phone."
- 16–22s  "One company improved the product." → "The other changed the future."
- 22–25s  End card: 🔴 pill + "Full story → today's carousel." + follow

## Music (added in-app)
Minimal cinematic · low piano · soft pulses · slow build. Investigative, not
emotional or motivational. Since there's no VO, music adds the tension — keep it under the text, not over it.
