BlackBerry never stopped improving.

Right up to the end, its keyboards got better, its email got faster, its security got stronger.

It still lost.

Because customers had stopped caring about keyboards, email speed, and enterprise security. They wanted touch, apps, and simplicity.

BlackBerry listened to its current customers.
Apple imagined its future customers.

The lesson isn't really about phones:

Companies rarely fail because they stop improving.
They fail because they improve what customers no longer value.

💬 What feature are you investing in today that your customers may stop caring about tomorrow?
