BlackBerry didn't lose to Apple. It lost to the future.

Right up to the end, its keyboards got better, its email got faster, its security got stronger.

It still lost.

BlackBerry thought people wanted better phones.
People wanted computers in their pockets.

Apple changed the question.
BlackBerry kept answering the old one.

The lesson isn't really about phones:

Companies don't fail because they stop improving.
They fail because they improve what customers no longer value.

💬 If your biggest customer disappeared tomorrow, which feature would you stop building?
