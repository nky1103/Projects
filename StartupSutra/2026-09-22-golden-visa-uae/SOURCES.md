# Sources — Money Moves / Golden Visa (2026-09-22) — VERIFIED

## The threshold: AED 2M -> 10-year Golden Visa
- Real estate investors owning property with a purchase value of at least AED 2M can apply for a 10-year renewable residence permit; spouse, children and parents can be sponsored.
- Property can be completed or off-plan worth at least AED 2M, and can be mortgaged (requires a bank NOC).

## The 2025 change: AED 1M down-payment minimum removed
- In 2025, authorities removed the previously interpreted minimum down-payment of AED 1M (or 50%) for Golden Visa property investors. Investors using mortgages and buying off-plan can qualify, provided the property meets or exceeds AED 2M on a DLD-recognized basis.
