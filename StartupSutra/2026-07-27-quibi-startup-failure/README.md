## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- Founded Aug 2018 in LA (as NewTV) by Jeffrey Katzenberg; CEO Meg Whitman.
- Raised $1.75B (Disney, NBCU, Sony, WarnerMedia, Alibaba, Goldman, JPMorgan).
- Product: <10-min "quick bite" episodes, mobile-only, paid ($4.99 ads / $7.99
  no ads), "Turnstyle" portrait+landscape.
- Launched 6 Apr 2020; announced shutdown 21 Oct 2020; ceased Dec 2020.
- ~910k free-trial downloads; 90%+ didn't convert. Library sold to Roku (Jan
  2021) for <$100M → free "Roku Originals".
Sources: CNBC, Wikipedia, The Verge, NBC News.
