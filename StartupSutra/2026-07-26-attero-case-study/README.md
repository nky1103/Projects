## Verified facts (publicly sourced)
- Founded 2008 (couldn't responsibly dump an old laptop → saw the gap). India's
  largest e-waste + Li-ion battery recycler; "urban mining."
- Recovers gold, copper, silver, aluminium + lithium, cobalt, nickel at ~99%
  purity. **46 granted global patents** (9 e-waste, ~37 Li-ion), 200+ filed.
- Only India facility handling all end-of-life Li-ion batteries; only such
  recycler globally generating **carbon credits**.
- Funding ~$31M (IndoUS/Kalaari, IFC, DFJ). Revenue FY23 ₹285 Cr → FY24 ₹440 Cr
  → FY25 >₹1,000 Cr (~100% YoY), cash-flow positive; **IPO-bound**, ~$2B revenue
  target by 2027; ₹8,300 Cr capex to 415,000 t/yr e-waste + 50,000 t/yr batteries.
- Tailwind: E-Waste Rules 2022 + EPR (60→70→80% mandates); market ~$3.3B (2025)
  → ~$10B (2034).
- Competitors: Lohum, Recykal, BatX, informal sector (~90% of e-waste).
Sources: Inc42, EVreporter, Business Standard, The Ken, Tracxn, company.
