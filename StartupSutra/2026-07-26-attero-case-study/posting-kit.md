1. Case Study, Attero Recycling. India's next gold mine isn't underground, it's in your drawer. Dead electronics in, gold, copper and lithium out.
2. The problem: India generates over 1.6 million tonnes of e-waste a year, around 90% handled informally, so critical minerals are lost and then re-imported.
3. The business model: dead electronics and batteries in, proprietary processing at 99% purity, recovered minerals out, sold to OEMs and battery makers as recurring revenue.
4. The opportunity: India's e-waste market grows from 3.3 to 10 billion dollars by 2034, EPR mandates reach 80% by FY28, and the EV boom needs lithium India imports.
5. Go-to-market: sell to those the law forces to recycle, via OEM partnerships, enterprise metal supply, a collection network (MetalMandi, SelSmart), and battery makers.
6. The framework: own the technology layer, not operations. Collection and sorting are commoditised; the patented 99% purity recovery step is the moat.
7. Competitive landscape: Attero (e-waste + batteries, 46 patents, carbon credits) vs Lohum (batteries), Recykal (marketplace), and the informal sector.
8. Why they won: technology (46 patents), timing (before the EV wave), regulation (EPR demand), execution (₹440 Cr to ₹1,000 Cr+, cash-flow positive).
9. Founder playbook: find opportunity in a broken industry, make the patent the moat, turn regulation into a tailwind, own the technology layer. Follow @statupsutra, Case Study on Sundays.
