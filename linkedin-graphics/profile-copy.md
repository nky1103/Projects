### Notes

- The About opens with the exact line from your post/banner, so anyone landing on
  the profile from the post sees one continuous message.
- Paragraph 2 uses only the industries you've named publicly. If you want
  specifics (years, markets, named outcomes), slot them into paragraph 2 —
  numbers belong there, not in the opening.
- "Modern tools" keeps the AI-enabled angle without saying "AI", per your
  restraint list. Say "AI" there instead if you'd rather be explicit.
- The closer reuses the banner/post line verbatim — that repetition is the point.
