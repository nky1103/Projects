01

Logo & mark

ClimaSeed
ClimaSeed

The mark is a sprout breaking from a seed-ring — the arc is the funding cycle, the amber node is capital, the two leaves are growth and impact. Use the full lockup where space allows; the standalone ring-sprout works as an app icon and favicon.

Clear space & sizing

Keep clear space equal to the height of the seed node on all sides. Minimum wordmark width 96px on screen; never render the mark below 20px.

Don't

Don't recolour outside the palette, stretch the mark, add drop shadows, rotate the sprout, or place the green wordmark on busy photography without a scrim.

04

Voice & tone

Grounded & optimistic — say
  • “We earn when you raise. No equity, ever.”
  • “Real operators, not just introductions.”
  • “Register your idea in three minutes.”
  • Plain, specific, credible. Numbers over adjectives.
Avoid
  • Hype and greenwashing (“revolutionary”, “world-changing”).
  • Jargon or finance-speak that excludes first-time founders.
  • Guarantees about funding outcomes.
  • Vague impact claims with no measure behind them.

Compliance note: success-fee facilitation of investment is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions (broker/intermediary rules — e.g. SEBI in India, broker-dealer rules in the US). Before charging the 5% fee, structure it through a licensed intermediary or with securities counsel. Services revenue (retainers) is unaffected and can launch immediately.
