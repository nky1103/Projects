Positioning

Where founders put down roots.

Ventroot is the launchpad and capital network for ambitious founders — across sectors. We vet real traction, get companies raise-ready with a bench of operators in finance, law, marketing and leadership, and route them into a growing network of investors, anchored by a partner writing $10M+ growth cheques.

01

How the network works

Step 1

Register & get vetted

Founders submit their venture — any sector. Ventroot screens for founder quality and real traction, not just an idea, before anything moves forward.

Step 2

Get raise-ready

The services bouquet — CA, legal, marketing, fractional COO — preps financials, cap table and story so the company can credibly absorb a large cheque.

Step 3

Matched to capital

Routed into the right relationship: the anchor investor for growth-ready or traction-backed deals ≥$10M, or a partner investor for earlier stage and other sectors.

02

Logo & mark

Ventroot
Ventroot

The mark encodes the name literally: a flame above — venture, ignition, upward growth — meets a root system below — foundation, branching into a network. The gold root-tip is the anchor deal; the green tips are the wider investor network across sectors.

Clear space & sizing

Keep clear space equal to the height of the central node on all sides. Minimum wordmark width 96px on screen; never render the mark below 20px.

Don't

Don't recolour outside the palette, stretch the mark, add drop shadows, flip the flame/root orientation, or place the mark on busy photography without a scrim.

05

Voice & tone

Grounded & direct — say
  • “We get paid a commission when you raise. No cash upfront.”
  • “Real operators and real capital, not just introductions.”
  • “One network — every sector but hospitality on the anchor track.”
  • Plain, specific, credible. Numbers over adjectives.
Avoid
  • Hype (“revolutionary”, “unicorn factory”).
  • Jargon or finance-speak that excludes first-time founders.
  • Guarantees about funding outcomes or valuations.
  • Implying Ventroot itself is an investor — it arranges, it doesn't invest.

Monthly retainer
Compliance note: a founder-paid, success-contingent commission on a capital raise is regulated intermediary activity in most jurisdictions (in India, SEBI merchant-banker / investment-adviser rules; broker-dealer rules in the US). Before charging the 5% fee on any deal, route it through a licensed entity — either register directly or partner with a SEBI-registered merchant banker — so the fee is enforceable and the deal is clean. Services revenue (retainers) carries no such constraint and can launch immediately.

08

Sectors

Sustainability & Climate — flagship vertical Fintech Healthtech Enterprise SaaS Consumer Deep tech Agritech Mobility Hospitality — anchor-track exclusion

Ventroot is broad by design. Sustainability remains the founding, flagship vertical; every other sector is open on the network — hospitality is excluded only on the anchor investor's track, and can still be routed to partner investors.

09

Name & availability

Ventroot = Venture (sector-neutral, capital, risk) + Root (foundation, network, growth). Coined, ownable, and — rare for a two-syllable brandable name — the bare .com is free. No existing company or prominent trademark surfaced in a first-pass search.
